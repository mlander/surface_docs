# Proposing a change

Much of what is on this site was decided after a long look at the alternatives, and the reasoning is written down. That reasoning is a starting point, not a wall. The aim is to make the design better, and that is easiest when a proposal builds on what was weighed before instead of weighing it again from the start.

## What a proposal says

1. **The decision it revisits**, with a link to its record.
2. **What is new since the decision.** A case it did not consider, a change in core, a measurement, a contributed module that does it another way.
3. **The facts that drove the decision.** Either the proposal keeps them, or it says why they no longer hold.
4. **The same test.** The change is tried on the same real cases the original was. See [How each piece is validated](../path/validation.md).

## Where it goes

Open an issue in the proof of concept's [issue queue](https://www.drupal.org/project/issues/surface) and link it from the talking point's page. If the proposal is accepted, the decision record gets a dated note, the page changes, and the talking point's level is set again.

## What is open without a proposal

Parts rated Working, Sketch or Question are open to review and redesign as they are. Their pages list what would help most.
